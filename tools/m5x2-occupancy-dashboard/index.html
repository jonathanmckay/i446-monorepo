Scheduled = leases already signed in the pipeline, bucketed by their move-in week — committed, and fed into the forecast as real move-ins. Long-term — same four states as % of portfolio, weekly since 2024

AppFolio's rented/unrented split is not a true as-of-then snapshot — it back-applies each unit's current rented flag to past dates, so historical vacant-unrented is undercounted (a unit vacant-unrented in April but leased since reads as vacant-rented in April). Only units & occupied are reliable, so all four bands are reconstructed: vacancy = units − occupied (real), split by today's live unrented share from the unit-level report; notice is estimated off occupied when the raw figures are implausible.

Occupancy newsfeed — daily tenant tickler

    Each row is dated by the real event date, newest first. Genuinely new notices and signings show their detection date (when our daily snapshot diff first saw them); rows marked baseline (pre-tracking) predate that diffing and have no detection date, so they are dated by their move date (effective) instead of all being stamped today. AppFolio exposes no signed/ received timestamp, so the effective date is the most truthful anchor for the backfill.
